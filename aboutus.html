About Us

Our Story & History

Established in Freetown with the sole purpose of creating a global presence, our agency is dedicated to the development, management and guidance of Sierra Leonean footballers. We commit ourselves to ensure players reach the highest standards both on and off the pitch.

The Football Agency Sierra Leone (FASL) was founded in 2025 by a group of passionate sports professionals and community leaders led by Joseph Barber. The agency was established in response to a critical gap in the Sierra Leonean football ecosystem: the lack of a structured, ethical and internationally connected body that could transition local talents from amateur leagues to the competitive demands of European, African or Asian clubs.

Timeline

2025 – Agency Founding

FASL is officially registered in Freetown.

2025 – First European Transfer

Joseph Barber secures a landmark deal with a top-division European club.

2025 – Community Program Launch

Annual Freetown Youth Football Festival is funded and kicked off.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be the leading football agency in West Africa, recognised globally for developing Sierra Leonean players and fostering excellence on and off the pitch.

Mission

To maximise player potential and reinvest into local youth programmes to ensure a continuous pipeline of talent for the future.

Our Achievements

  • Secured multiple Sierra Leonean transfers to top-tier European leagues (Belgian Pro League, Turkish Süper Lig).
  • Players represent Sierra Leone in AFCON qualifiers, occupying starting roles secured by the agency.
  • Fund and organise the annual Freetown Youth Football Festival, providing scouting opportunities for over 500 youngsters.
